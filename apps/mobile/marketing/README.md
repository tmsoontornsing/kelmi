## Store listing

Kelmi launches in the **United States only**, for **ages 13 and up**, free with an optional **Kelmi Plus** subscription. In App Store Connect and Google Play Console restrict availability to the United States. Do not state a specific age rating as fact anywhere in the listing: answer the App Store age rating questionnaire truthfully, and make sure the rating it produces is consistent with the app's actual content. In Google Play Console, set the target audience to 13+ and do not select any age group under 13 (do not include children under 13 in the audience selection). See `docs/legal-review.md`. Add Kelmi Plus as auto-renewable in-app purchases in App Store Connect and Google Play so the stores list them. Any listing text, screenshot or poster that names a Plus feature or price must say it is part of a paid subscription, and must match the price in the stores.

**Name:** Kelmi: Meal Plans on a Budget

**Subtitle (30 characters max):** Weekly meals around your budget

**Promotional text:**
Meet Pip, the jar that plans your week. Tell Kelmi who you cook for and what you spend, and Pip fills the week with meals that fit, plus one grocery list to shop from.

**Description:**
Kelmi plans every meal of your week around your grocery budget.

Tell Pip, Kelmi's jar, who you cook for, what you spend, and what everyone eats. Pip plans breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the week and shows an estimated cost before you buy a thing.

- Plans around your budget and diet. It filters out ingredients you list; always check labels.
- Puts the foods you love first. Like curry and pasta? They show up first.
- Builds one grocery list for the whole week. What you already have in your pantry stays off it.
- Swap any meal you are not feeling for one that still fits the week (Kelmi Plus).

Kelmi is free, with three meal plans a month. Kelmi Plus, an optional auto-renewing subscription, adds a plan every week and more; cancel any time in your store settings. For ages 13 and up in the United States. Prices and nutrition are estimates, not advice.

Kelmi is not a medical tool. The ingredient filter is a helper, not a guarantee: recipe tags can be wrong, so always read product labels, and talk to a doctor about food allergies.
