# Kelmi Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This policy is for people in the United States and is provided by LOGICBAKER COMPANY LIMITED, a company registered in Thailand at [REGISTERED ADDRESS] ("we", "us"), under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law. It adds detail to our Privacy Policy. It is not medical advice, and Kelmi is not a medical service.

## 1. What consumer health data we collect

- Allergies and foods you must avoid.
- Your diet (for example vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, lower carb or halal).
- Foods you like and dislike.
- Food notes you type in Settings (for example "low sugar" or "easy on the stomach"), which are free text and may describe a health condition if you choose to write one.
- Your eating goal and meat preference, and the nutrition estimates shown for your meals.

We collect this only from you, when you enter it. We do not collect it from other sources. Before Kelmi plans meals with it, the app asks for your consent on a separate screen (the allergy notice, "I understand and agree"), and we record when you gave it.

Kelmi is for people 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information; if we learn we have, we delete it. People 13 to 17 may use Kelmi, and their consumer health data is handled the same way as everyone else's under this policy. We do not sell it, and it goes to an AI provider only if the person allows it in the app. Laws on minors' health-related data vary by state, and we do not claim this policy meets all of them.

## 2. Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the service you ask for: to choose meals that avoid the foods you list and fit your diet, goal and budget, to build your grocery list, and to show you your own settings. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## 3. Who we share it with

- Supabase, our database and hosting provider, which stores it for us as a service provider.
- AI providers (Anthropic and IDUnify AI), only if you allow it. Kelmi asks before anything is sent. The in-app screen says: "To pick your meals, Kelmi sends your household size, budget, allergies, diet, likes, dislikes and food notes to an AI provider (Anthropic or IDUnify AI). It does not send your name or email. You can say no and Kelmi will use its built-in planner instead." If you say no, Kelmi uses its built-in planner and sends nothing to an AI provider. We do not send your name or email, but food notes are free text, so only type what you are comfortable sending. We have not yet confirmed either provider's retention or model-training terms in a signed agreement, so we make no promise about them.
- Government or other parties only when the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell consumer health data and we do not share it for advertising. We do not collect it for any purpose other than those in section 2.

Optional usage counts (see the Privacy Policy) never include your allergies, dislikes, diet, or food notes.

## 4. Your rights

- Withdraw consent. You can stop AI sharing in the app's Settings at any time. You can also leave allergy, diet and notes blank, or remove them, which withdraws your consent for them.
- Access. Ask us to confirm whether we collect your consumer health data, and for a copy of it.
- Delete. Delete it yourself in Settings, delete your whole account in Settings (Delete account), or ask us to delete it.
- Know who received it. Ask us for a list of the third parties and affiliates that received your consumer health data.
- Appeal. If we deny a request, you can appeal, and if you are not satisfied you can contact your state attorney general.

To make a request, email support@usekelmi.com from the address on your account. We will respond within 45 days, and may extend that once by up to 45 more days if we tell you why.

## 5. How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete it from our active systems, and copies in backups are removed on our database provider's backup cycle, which we expect to be up to about 30 days.

## 6. Security and changes

We use encryption in transit and access controls that limit each account to its own data. We will update this policy if our practices change, and we will ask for your consent before collecting or sharing more consumer health data than described here, where the law requires it.
