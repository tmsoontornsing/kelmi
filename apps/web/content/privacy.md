# Kelmi Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information LOGICBAKER COMPANY LIMITED ("we", "us") collects through the Kelmi app, the usekavi.com website and related services ("Kelmi"), how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. Kelmi is offered only in the United States and only to people 13 or older. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy gives more detail about the diet and allergy information you enter.

## 1. Information you give us

- Account. Your email address, and your password if you sign up with email (we store only a secure hash of it). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead) and a user ID, and Google may also share your name and profile picture.
- Household profile. How many people you cook for, your weekly grocery budget, your diet (for example vegetarian or halal), allergies, foods you dislike, foods you like, your goal and meat preference, any food notes you type in Settings (for example “low sugar”), and the kitchen equipment you have.
- Your lists. Pantry items, meal plans, meals you swap, which meals you choose to shop for (and which you skip, like nights eating out), grocery lists and what you check off, leftover meals and how many servings are left, and steps you mark done.
- Recipe edits. If you change a recipe, Kelmi saves your own version to your account: the amounts you changed, ingredients you left out, steps you rewrote, and any note you add. The original recipe is not changed.
- Meal ratings. The stars you give a meal after cooking it, kept private to your account. If you share a meal, Kelmi draws a picture on your phone with the dish name, your stars and the Kelmi name, and hands it to your phone's share sheet. It contains nothing about you, and Kelmi does not see where you send it.
- Preferences. Settings such as reminder and notification choices.
- Reminders (only if you choose "Add list to Reminders" on iPhone). Kelmi asks for permission to use Reminders, then adds your grocery items to a list named Kelmi Groceries. Kelmi reads only that list, to avoid adding an item twice, and never reads your other reminders. Those reminders live on your device and in your Apple account, under Apple's privacy practices; they are not sent to us. You can turn the permission off in iPhone Settings.
- Stores near you (only if you tap the location button on the grocery list). Kelmi asks you to confirm, then opens your phone's maps app (Apple Maps or Google Maps) with a search for grocery stores. Kelmi does not ask for, read, or store your location, and does not send it anywhere. Your maps app uses your location under its own privacy policy and your phone's location settings.
- Support. What you send us when you contact us.

## 2. Information collected automatically, and what we do not collect

Our hosting and sign-in providers record technical information needed to run and secure Kelmi, such as your IP address, device and app version, and the times you sign in or make requests. We also keep a record of each meal plan request (your account ID, the time, and which provider and how much computing it used) so we can apply the monthly limit and prevent abuse.

Usage counts (optional, off unless you turn them on). If you switch on "Share anonymous usage counts" in Settings, Kelmi records when you open the app, when a meal plan is made, and when a grocery list is built, together with a random number kept on your phone. It never includes your name, email, allergies, diet, food notes, recipes, or anything you type. The counts are stored in our own database, with Supabase as our service provider, are used only to see which features get used and to fix what does not work, are deleted when you delete your account, and are never sold or shared for advertising. You can turn them off at any time and collection stops right away. Kelmi does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking software from other companies, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If this changes, we will update this Policy and tell you before it takes effect, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

## 3. Sensitive information

Your allergies, dislikes, diet, and typed food notes can reveal information about your health or, in the case of a choice such as halal, your religious beliefs. Some laws treat this as sensitive or "consumer health" data. We collect it only because you choose to give it, and use it only to plan your meals and grocery lists. You can leave it blank, change it, or delete it in Settings at any time. We never sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## 4. AI meal planning

Kelmi can use an AI provider to pick your meals. It does this only after you allow it in the app. The screen in the app says: "To pick your meals, Kelmi sends your household size, budget, allergies, diet, likes, dislikes and food notes to an AI provider (Anthropic or IDUnify AI). It does not send your name or email. You can say no and Kelmi will use its built-in planner instead."

- What is sent. Your household size, weekly budget, allergies, diet, foods you like and dislike, and food notes, along with your eating goal and meat preference and a list of candidate recipes (recipe names, times and estimated costs) to choose from. We do not send your name, email address, or account ID. Food notes are free text, so do not type anything there that you would not want sent to an AI provider.
- Who receives it. Anthropic and IDUnify AI. They receive this information from us to produce a meal plan and act on our behalf as service providers (processors). We have not yet confirmed either provider's data retention or model-training terms in a signed agreement, so we make no promise about them here; we will update this Policy when we have. Each provider's own terms and privacy policy also apply.
- If you say no. Kelmi uses its built-in planner, which runs without an AI provider and sends nothing to one. Plans from the built-in planner may be simpler.
- Changing your mind. You can withdraw your permission at any time in the app's Settings. After that, Kelmi stops sending your information to an AI provider for new plans. Information already sent is handled under the provider's terms; ask us at the contact address below if you want help with that.

## 5. How we use information

- To provide Kelmi: plan meals that fit your household, budget, diet, allergies, and kitchen; estimate prices and nutrition; and build and sync your grocery list.
- To manage your account.
- To send notifications you turn on, and service messages such as changes to these policies.
- To respond to you, fix problems, and keep Kelmi secure, including preventing fraud and abuse.
- To improve Kelmi using information that does not identify you.
- To comply with the law and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to show you ads.

## 6. Who we share it with

- Supabase, our database, sign-in and hosting provider, which stores your account and data for us as a service provider.
- AI providers (Anthropic and IDUnify AI), only if you allow it. See section 4.
- Apple and Google. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they are independent companies that decide for themselves how to handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies; they are not our service providers. If we later add paid plans, Apple or Google will likewise be independent controllers of payment information.
- Maps apps you choose to open. When you tap the grocery store button, your phone hands a search for "grocery store" to Apple Maps or Google Maps. We send them no personal information, and they are not our service providers; their own privacy policies apply.
- For legal reasons: when required by law or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of you, others, or us.
- In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Account and profile data (email, household profile, pantry, plans, lists, ratings, recipe edits): kept while your account exists. We delete it from our active systems when you delete your account in Settings.
- Meal plan request records: kept while your account exists, and deleted with it.
- Backups: copies of deleted data in backups are removed on our database provider's backup cycle, which we expect to be up to about 30 days.
- Waitlist emails: kept until Kelmi launches and we send the launch email, or until you ask us to remove you, and then deleted within a reasonable time (see section 9).
- Server logs: kept by our hosting providers for the periods they set, normally short.
- Records the law requires us to keep, or that we need to resolve a dispute, may be kept longer. Apple and Google keep their own sign-in records under their own policies.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- Change your details any time in Settings, and turn notifications on or off there or in your device settings.
- Delete your account and its data: Settings, Delete account.
- Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing support@usekelmi.com.

Kelmi is offered only in the United States for now. Depending on the state where you live, such as California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Washington, Nevada, and others, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold and how we use it, to access and receive a portable copy, to correct it, to delete it, to limit the use of sensitive information, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of sale, sharing for advertising, and profiling. We offer these choices to everyone who uses Kelmi, whether or not the law of your state requires it. Because we do not sell or share personal information for advertising, there is nothing to opt out of; if that ever changes, we will honor Global Privacy Control signals. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

To make a request, email support@usekelmi.com from the address on your account. We will verify your request and respond within 45 days. Where the law allows, we may extend that once by up to 45 more days if we tell you why. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond to your appeal within 60 days, or sooner if the law requires. If you are not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general.

## 9. The website and the waitlist

- Waitlist. If you join the waitlist on usekavi.com, we collect your email address and the time you joined. We use it only to send you one email when Kelmi is available. We do not use it for anything else and do not sell or share it for advertising. The form has a link to this Policy.
- Getting off the waitlist. Email support@usekelmi.com and ask us to remove you, or use the unsubscribe link in any email we send you. We will delete your address.
- Hosting and logs. The website is served by a hosting provider, which, like most hosts, records technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time, in server logs, to deliver and secure the site. We use this only for running and protecting the site.
- Cookies and storage. The website sets no cookies. If you use the light/dark toggle, it saves your choice in your browser's local storage under the name "kavi-theme". This stays on your device and is not sent to us. The website has no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts.

## 10. Consumer health data and state laws

The allergies, dislikes, diet, and food notes you enter may be consumer health data under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law. Our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights, including the right to withdraw consent and to delete it. In short: we collect it from you, use it only to provide the meal planning you ask for, share it only with our service providers (and with the AI providers named in section 4 only if you allow it), and never sell it.

## 11. Where Kelmi is offered

Kelmi is offered only in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. We do not offer Kelmi in the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Thailand or any other country, and we do not design this Policy to meet those countries' laws. If you are outside the United States, please do not use Kelmi or give us your information. If we expand to other countries, we will publish a policy for them first.

## 12. Children

Kelmi is not directed to, and is not knowingly used by, children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (see the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA). If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact support@usekelmi.com and we will delete it. People aged 13 to 17 may use Kelmi (the Terms ask those under 18 to use it only with a parent or guardian's permission, which Kelmi does not verify). We handle their information under this Policy like everyone else's: we do not sell it, we do not show ads, and we send food preferences to an AI provider only after the person allows it in the app. Some state laws give minors extra privacy protections, and we do not claim that this Policy meets all of them.

## 13. Security

We protect your information with measures such as encryption in transit, access controls that limit each account to its own data, and keeping secret keys off your device. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

## 14. Changes to this Policy

We will update this Policy when our practices change. If a change is material, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
